How it works

From a crumpled receipt to a clear month, in three steps

Snap it

Upload a photo of any printed receipt. The image is straightened and cleaned, then the text is read on the server.

Check it

The shop, date, total and a likely category are filled in for you. Fix anything that looks off, then save.

See it

The expense appears on its day. Your month total, budget and what you have saved update straight away.

Privacy

Your receipts stay with you

Receipts are read by models running on the same server as the app, not sent to an outside text-reading service.

  • Private accountsEvery account sees only its own expenses, budgets and receipts.
  • Passwords are never stored as plain textThey are salted and hashed, and sessions expire after 14 days.
  • Yours to take with youExport any month as a CSV file, and delete any expense whenever you like.

Questions

Good to know

What kind of receipts work best?

Printed receipts photographed flat, in good light and straight on. Handwriting, heavy folds and glare are harder to read, so always check the details before saving.

What if it reads something wrong?

You always see the details first. Change the shop, amount, date or category, then save. Nothing is added until you confirm it.

How do the personal and business profiles work?

They belong to one account but keep separate expenses, budgets and totals. Switch between them at the top of the page.

Can I see a whole year at once?

Yes. The Year tab shows all twelve months, your yearly total, your monthly average and your spending by category. Tap a month to jump to its calendar.

Which currency does it use?

The rupee by default. The symbol is one setting in the page, so you can change it to any currency you like.
